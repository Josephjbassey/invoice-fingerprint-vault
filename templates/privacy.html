Privacy Policy &
Data Minimization Standard

Invoice Fingerprint Vault is architected from first principles under the doctrine of absolute data minimization. We believe the safest data is the data that is never collected, stored, or transmitted.

Zero-Upload Client Processing

When using the client-side hasher or public verification portal, your documents (PDF, JPG, PNG) are processed in your local browser memory via the W3C Web Crypto API. The raw binary never leaves your computer.

One-Way Preimage Security

Only the 32-byte SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprint is transmitted to the Gno.land blockchain. It is mathematically impossible to reconstruct invoice balances, customer addresses, line items, or tax identifiers from this hash.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Document Contents: We do not store invoice bodies, line items, customer identities, payment amounts, or proprietary trade agreements.
  • No Banking or Payment Credentials: We never handle credit cards, bank account numbers, SWIFT/IBAN identifiers, or custodial wallet private keys.
  • No Tracking Pixels or Ad Telemetry: We do not integrate Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or behavioral tracking SDKs.

2. Information We Process

Depending on your mode of interaction, we process only the bare technical minimum required to perform decentralized consensus:

  • Cryptographic Hashes: 64-character hexadecimal SHA-256 strings submitted to anchor document existence.
  • Public Blockchain Addresses: Bech32 Gno wallet addresses (g1...) when signing transactions through Adena wallet.
  • Voluntary Account Credentials: If you choose to register for an optional hybrid vault account, we store a username and salt-hashed password (via PBKDF2/Argon2) for your personal dashboard.

3. Public Ledger Transparency & Immutability

Please be aware that any transaction broadcast to the Gno.land network (onyx-1 or mainnet) becomes part of a public, decentralized, and immutable ledger. While your document remains 100% private and undisclosed, the fingerprint and the signing address are publicly visible on-chain.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance by Design

Because no Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is committed to the blockchain, Invoice Fingerprint Vault is inherently compliant with global data privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Article 25 (Data Protection by Design and by Default) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use strictly necessary first-party session cookies exclusively for user authentication when logged in, and CSRF protection tokens to prevent cross-site request forgery attacks. No marketing or tracking cookies are ever deployed.
