1. Nature of the Service (Non-Custodial Proof-of-Existence)

Invoice Fingerprint Vault provides a decentralized cryptographic notary service. Submitting a document generates a mathematical SHA-256 fingerprint that is recorded in an on-chain AVL tree on the Gno.land blockchain.

The smart contract proves existence and data integrity at a specific block timestamp. It does not constitute legal title, governmental licensing, or automated settlement of invoices.

2. User Custody of Original Files

Because Invoice Fingerprint Vault enforces strict Zero-Knowledge principles, we do not store or host your original invoice files.

You are solely responsible for archiving and preserving the original unmodified documents. Because cryptographic hashes are one-way functions, lost documents cannot be recovered from their hash under any circumstances.
